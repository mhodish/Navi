Europe GPS Roadbook V18.9.10.2 — Uig Overnight Hub

Changes from V18.9.10.1

1. Uig overnight POIs are now visibly represented on the strip as a local hub around Ferry Inn instead of existing only as off-route detail cards.
2. Added keyed local markers U1–U6 and matching labels in the POI cards:
   U1 Fairy Glen
   U2 Rha Falls
   U3 Uig Community Woodland
   U4 Uig Tower / Captain Fraser's Folly
   U5 Uig Pottery
   U6 Skye Museum of Island Life
3. The local hub convention deliberately groups off-route attractions at the overnight stop; their strip position is not intended to imply that they lie on the through-road.
4. Corrected the temporary Uig background-audio test implementation. The two test zones are now checked directly from the proven live GPS pos() path, without changing arrival/departure/navigation state.
   - Uig south test: 57.5740, -6.3380
   - Uig north test: 57.6070, -6.3520
   - Trigger radius: 300 m
5. Test-zone fired state uses a new V18.9.10.2 localStorage key so both tests are fresh for this build.
6. Service-worker/cache version bumped to V18.9.10.2.
7. Navigation constants and proven arrival/auto-advance engine otherwise unchanged.
8. Neist Point remains removed. Tineke Bloemen funeral remains fixed at 13:00 Sat 26 Sep.

Known limitation retained from V18.9.10.1:
Photographs are still remote/lazy and are not yet physically bundled in this update ZIP. The next photo-architecture build will package compressed local image files for guaranteed first-use offline viewing.
