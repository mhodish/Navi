Europe GPS Roadbook V18.9.10.5.6.5 — Clean Explore + startup/update repair

Built from 10.5.6.2, retaining its corrected geographic blue-dot mapping.

- On page open, requests one high-accuracy GPS fix for display and centers the normal strip on the blue dot.
  Navigation does not need to be started for this initial strip positioning.
- Explore no longer resizes or transforms the live Roadbook strip. It uses a temporary cloned SVG in a
  dedicated full-screen viewport.
- Explore opens at 1x centered on the live blue dot; drag pans; pinch scales proportionally around the
  midpoint between the fingers.
- POI tap in Explore passes only the POI key back to the normal Roadbook, closes Explore, and reveals the
  existing real POI information card for 10 seconds.
- Service worker update lifecycle made deterministic: new worker uses skipWaiting + clients.claim;
  online navigation/index is network-first with no-store, offline falls back to the complete cache;
  old Roadbook caches are removed on activation.
- 10.5.5 field-proven warning/arrival/departure/auto-advance navigation logic remains unchanged.
