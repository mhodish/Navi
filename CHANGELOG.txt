Europe GPS Roadbook V18.9.10.5.6 — Expandable Strip

Built from field-proven V18.9.10.5.5 Glasgow Walking Diagnostic baseline.

Changes:
- Added temporary Strip Explore mode for dense POI areas.
- EXPAND STRIP button opens the strip nearly full-screen, including phone portrait where the normal strip is hidden.
- Pinch with two fingers zooms the schematic strip from 1x to 4x, spreading dense POIs for selection.
- The expanded strip can be panned/scrollled normally.
- Tapping a POI retains the existing POI reveal/highlight behavior, then returns to the normal Roadbook layout.
- DONE (or Escape on keyboard) exits without selecting a POI.
- Pinching the visible normal strip also enters Explore mode.
- Navigation state, GPS, 2-minute warning, arrival latch, departure confirmation, and field-proven 10.5.5 auto-advance logic are unchanged.
- Offline cache/version advanced to 18.9.10.5.6.

Field-proven baseline preserved: V18.9.10.5.5.
